================================================================================
BACKUP AND RECOVERY
================================================================================

Backup storage is warnetech-backups.

Recovery steps:
  1. Export NDJSON from warnetech-server
  2. Store in warnetech-backups
  3. Rehydrate into warnetech-project-supabase
  4. Validate row counts and checksums
  5. Rebuild signature cache in warnetech-control-plane

Verification endpoint is hosted on warnetech-server.

================================================================================
